# v37.1.0 – Mobile Experience Redesign

A dedicated phone experience rather than a compressed desktop layout.

- Five-item bottom navigation: Home, Live, Movies, Series and More.
- Live TV rebuilt for phones with player-first layout, group bottom sheet, search, EPG-rich channel rows and quality badges.
- Home tuned for thumb use with swipe rails and compact Live Now cards.
- Movies and Series use a two-column touch grid, sticky compact filters and mobile detail layouts.
- Mobile Library Management/Admin uses cards and scroll-safe navigation instead of desktop-width tables.
- Mobile sheets lock background scrolling correctly.
- Desktop, tablet and TV rendering paths remain separate.

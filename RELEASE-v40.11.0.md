# MyOnlineTV v40.11.0 — Movies & Series UX

Focus: make movie resume and episodic viewing feel continuous without compromising playback reliability.

## Changes
- Player-level Resume / Start Over choice for saved positions of 30 seconds or more.
- 10-second Up Next autoplay countdown after an episode.
- Play now and Cancel controls for Up Next.
- Watched state is saved before transitioning to the next episode.
- Responsive mobile treatment for resume and Up Next controls.
- No heuristic Skip Intro/Credits markers are invented; support will be added when reliable metadata is available.
